# Responsible Use Policy

Use this project only for lawful, authorized and transparent automation.

## Allowed

- Product tutorials, training, documentation and quality assurance.
- Recording accounts and systems controlled by the operator.
- Reproducible demonstrations using demo data and authorized sessions.
- Accessibility, testing and internal workflow documentation.

## Not allowed

- Accessing accounts, systems or data without authorization.
- Credential harvesting, session theft, phishing or deceptive login flows.
- Bypassing CAPTCHAs, paywalls, access controls, rate limits or anti-abuse systems.
- Recording or publishing personal, financial, medical or confidential information without a valid legal basis and appropriate consent.
- Automating conduct that violates applicable law or the target service's binding terms.
- Presenting synthetic or edited demonstrations as authentic evidence when that would mislead viewers.

## Required safeguards

- Use demo accounts whenever possible.
- Keep live mode behind explicit human approval.
- Review all outputs before publication.
- Remove or blur private data.
- Stop when a site requests additional verification or blocks automation; do not attempt evasion.
